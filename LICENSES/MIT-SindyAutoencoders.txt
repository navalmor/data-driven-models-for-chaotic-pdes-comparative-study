The AE-SINDy implementation in ae_sindy/ is a PyTorch reimplementation of the
TensorFlow reference implementation released as kpchamp/SindyAutoencoders with
Champion, Lusch, Kutz and Brunton, "Data-driven discovery of coordinates and
governing equations", PNAS 116(45):22445-22451, 2019.
